Profit before tax by program
Program results

Tuition and teaching costs (the Excel “PHAR” and “MED” academic lines) belong to each program. All other income and costs are shared in the Excel, so they are split by each program’s share of tuition. The two columns add up to the totals in the P&L statement. This is an estimate: splitting by student numbers or teaching hours would give different results.

Source: P&L 2025 2026.xlsb, sheet “P&L”. Figures are cash-based (amounts collected and paid in the year), recorded in West African CFA francs (FCFA, the currency of Senegal) and shown in euros by default. FCFA is fixed to the euro at 655.957 FCFA per €1, so EUR figures are exact. USD figures use one rate for all years, €1 = $1.12 (market rate on 11 Oct 2026); they are a convenience view, not historical dollar amounts.

Academic years run from 2008-09. 2026-27 is in progress: only tuition collected so far is recorded and no expenses have been booked yet, so it is shown separately and excluded from trend charts.
